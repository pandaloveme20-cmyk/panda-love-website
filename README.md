# Panda Love — Happiness by the Cup

Panda Love – Happiness by the Cup. Sri Lanka's playful, happy bubble tea brand serving boba shakes, milk tea, matcha, fruit teas and ice cream. Explore 22 flavours, signature drinks and store formats, and visit us in Bambalapitiya, Savoy Wellawatta and Negombo, with Malabe coming soon.

## Site
- `index.html` — the whole website (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file)
- `assets/` — logo, drink photos and store photos
- `video/` — hero video, intro film and flavour panel videos
